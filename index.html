CONCEPT / PRE-FEASIBILITY ONLY Uses fixed source-to-HMP and HMP-to-branch ΔT losses and a simplified network return-temperature reference. No hydraulic pressure simulation, flow-dependent pipe heat transfer, return-network mixing, dynamic thermal storage, or detailed pump-electricity model. Heat pumps use simplified constant COP; no compressor-map capacity derating. HMP temperature capability and thermal capacity are separate; no mixed HMP temperature is calculated in strict mode. GridBooster compressor electricity is additional electric input; its heat is not credited against upstream production. Experimental blending estimates source mixing from power and one common return reference using constant cp and density. No hydraulic mixing, source pump curves, pressure balance, hydraulic dispatch, transient ramping, thermal inertia or plant efficiency maps are modeled. Plant ramp cost is a simplified variable penalty.
